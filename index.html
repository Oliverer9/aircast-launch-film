AirCast Iceland launch film

A 40-second film that launches AirCast Iceland, a live map of Iceland's air quality and wind. Made with Claude Opus 5.5 and HyperFrames: every scene is a web page on a timeline, rendered frame by frame to MP4.

  • 40 s · 720p
  • Voice: Kokoro "Heart", made on my own computer
  • Real site and real data, no made-up readings
  • 1080p version (18 MB)
