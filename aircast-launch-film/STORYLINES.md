# Storylines

Three different films (each its own world, device and form). Recommended first.
Picked: **A** (Oliver chose pitch 1, "Living map", 30.10.2026).

## A. The living map (recommended, picked)
- **Device:** the result first. **Form:** a flight through one world (AirCast's own map),
  with object-to-object hand-offs into the real interface.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** Iceland's map is already alive at frame 0, wind
  streaks moving over the real AirCast map. One station, Grensás in Reykjavík, is yellow:
  the film follows that one real reading from the dot, to "Moderate", to the reason
  (nitrogen dioxide, 25.1 µg/m³), to the wind that will carry it away.
- **First two seconds:** the real map, streaks already moving; "Is the air OK right now?"
  lands at 0.3 s.
- **Beats:** hook (map alive) → tension (two sources that don't share a station) →
  reveal (AirCast Iceland) → proof (click Grensás, "Moderate", why, last 24 h) →
  payoff (the wind, pushed in on Reykjavík) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** the site's own dark navy, Inter, its level colours; the real map tiles.
- **Sound:** see SPEC.md (to be decided with Oliver).

## B. The morning run
- **Device:** a moment in someone's day. **Form:** object-to-object chain.
- A phone alarm at 07:00, a pair of running shoes by the door, the phone opens AirCast
  (mobile layout), the badge says "Good", the door opens onto the street map. Warm, human.
- **First two seconds:** the phone screen at 07:00 with the question "Run today?".
- **Look:** a light morning world, the phone as the carrier. **Sound:** quiet morning bed.

## C. One number (the bold one)
- **Device:** a number lands. **Form:** one frame that rebuilds.
- "25.1" fills the frame. The film earns it: traffic lines draw across Reykjavík, the
  number is NO₂ at Grensás, the colour scale snaps under it to "Moderate", then the frame
  rebuilds into the site.
- **First two seconds:** "25.1" huge, in yellow, ticking up from 0.
- **Look:** giant type in Inter 900 on the site's navy. **Sound:** a tight pulse, cuts on the beat.
